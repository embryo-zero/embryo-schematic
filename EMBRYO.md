# Embryo Schematic

Embryo Schematic is Embryo-Zero's fork of [Xschem](https://github.com/StefanSchippers/xschem) (beb7bcb, commit beb7bcb). It draws transistor-level circuits and writes simulation netlists.
It is one of the Embryo chip tools, with Embryo Router (our Freerouting fork) and Embryo PCB (our KiCad fork).

- Our changes live on the `embryo` branch. The original license files (LICENSE) are unchanged.
- Legal notes: LEGAL.md. Dated list of our changes: CHANGES-EMBRYO.md.
